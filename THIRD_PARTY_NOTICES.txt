  UEVR plugin API (praydog/UEVR, API.h)     MIT                     all six plugins

  miniz (Rich Geldreich, RAD Game Tools,    MIT                     GlobalSettings (reads the game's .pak for
         Valve Software)                                            PAPERDOLLS > EXTRACT)

  Vulkan-Headers (The Khronos Group)        Apache-2.0 OR MIT       VoiceCommand (Whisper on the graphics card;

                                                                    apps\onnxruntime_providers_shared.dll
  MW5-UEVR-Plugins (sicsix)                 MIT                     the project this one grew from (LICENSE.txt)

Models and data
  Whisper base.en model (OpenAI), ggml conversion          MIT          apps\models\ggml-base.en.bin
  Kokoro-82M multi-language (hexgrad), sherpa-onnx export  Apache-2.0   apps\kokoro-82m\kokoro-multi-lang-v1_0
      (with that export's lexicons and dictionaries)
  espeak-ng data (inside the Kokoro folder)                GPL-3.0      apps\kokoro-82m\...\espeak-ng-data
      espeak-ng is free software under the GNU GPL v3; its source and licence are at
      https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng

Game-derived data
  apps\assets\dropships (hull outlines of the Leopard, Union and Broadsword dropships) are traced from
  meshes in MechWarrior 5: Mercenaries and its DLC, (c) Piranha Games Inc. They are included only so the MFD
  screens can draw the game's own dropships, for owners of the game, and are not covered by this project's
  MIT licence.
  The mech paperdolls are NOT shipped: GlobalSettings > PAPERDOLLS > EXTRACT makes them on your own PC from
  your own copy of the game.
